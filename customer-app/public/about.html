About JothidaConnect

JothidaConnect is operated by slakitservice.

JothidaConnect connects you with independent, experienced astrologers for personal consultations by chat and voice call. Ask about love, marriage, career, finance, family or personal growth, from wherever you are.

What we offer

  • Chat consultations: text conversations with the astrologer you choose.
  • Voice call consultations: speak directly with an astrologer.
  • Birth chart preparation: your astrologer sees your rasi and navamsam charts and dasa/bhukti periods, prepared from the birth details you provide.
  • Daily horoscope: a free reading for your zodiac sign.
  • A choice of astrologers: compare skills, languages, experience, ratings and rates.

How it works

  • Create an account with your mobile number and add your birth details.
  • Add money to your wallet or buy a minutes package.
  • Choose an available astrologer and request a chat or call. You need balance for at least 5 minutes at that astrologer's rate.
  • Billing starts only when the astrologer accepts, and is charged per minute. The first minute is charged at the start.
  • The consultation ends automatically if your balance runs out.
